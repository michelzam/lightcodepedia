# 👋 01 · Introductions

🛢️ Data lives somewhere. You are about to find out where.

This first module sets the scene for the whole course — who your
instructor is, how the course works, and why databases are worth a
semester of your life.

**The promise:** by the end of this module you can point at a table and
say exactly which row wins — in English, and in SQL.

**Three walks, in order:**

1. [🗽 Welcome to 410](01_welcome.md) — the course, the outcomes, the
   grading, the rules of the road.
2. [🏆 The Data Quest](02_data_quest.md) — a story about cranberry
   sauce, an award, and one Thomas Hardy of London. Your first real
   query hides inside.
3. [📋 Databases & RDBMS Concepts](03_rdbms_concepts.md) — the words
   for what you just did: database, DBMS, the relational model.

Each page has a guide — press play and Doc walks you through, or read
at your own pace. When you are done, the **Welcome Quiz** waits in
Canvas, Module 01.
